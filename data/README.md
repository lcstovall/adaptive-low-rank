## Datasets

The experiments use the following datasets:

- Interactions
- MNIST
- MovieLens
- COIL-20
- YearPredictionMSD

Due to their distribution/licensing terms, the datasets are not included in this repository.

The following datasets must be downloaded to reproduce our results:

- MovieLens (rating.csv)
- COIL-20 (coil-20-proc/)
- YearPredictionMSD (YearPredictionMSD.txt)

Place the downloaded datasets in:

data/
